Why Join Us?

ORION is a community of students passionate about technology, innovation, and building impactful products. Join us to learn, build, and grow alongside like-minded peers.

Work on Real Engineering Projects

Gain hands-on experience by contributing to projects that solve real-world problems.

QO-100 Satellite

Build and operate a satellite communication system from Kerala that connects to the QO-100 geostationary amateur satellite. Learn about RF communication, antennas, SDRs, microwave engineering, and satellite networks.

CanSat Mission

Design and build a miniature satellite the size of a soft drink can. Work on sensors, embedded systems, telemetry, wireless communication, and data analysis while experiencing the complete satellite development lifecycle.

Full-Stack Platform

Contribute to a production-ready web application used to manage club memberships, events, projects, attendance, and communications. Gain experience with modern software development, UI/UX, databases, APIs, deployment, and teamwork.

Career & Internships

Our members don't just learn—they achieve results.

Industry Exposure
